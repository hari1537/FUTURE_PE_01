📌 Patient Symptom Example

Reason for Visit:
I am suffering from fever for the past 3 days, which may indicate an infection or underlying disease. Therefore, I need to visit the clinic for proper diagnosis and treatment.

🧠 Prompt Used

Write a short patient statement describing a health issue for a clinic website. Include a symptom (fever), duration (3 days), and reason for visiting the clinic. Keep it simple and clear.

🎯 Purpose

This section demonstrates how patients can clearly describe their symptoms, helping doctors understand their condition better and provide accurate treatment.
